Why add water?

Hot magnesium also reacts with nitrogen in the air and forms a little magnesium nitride, Mg₃N₂. That would give the wrong product mass.

Water turns the nitride into magnesium hydroxide, and ammonia gas escapes:

Mg₃N₂ + 6H₂O → 3Mg(OH)₂ + 2NH₃

Heating then turns the hydroxide into the oxide and drives off the water:

Mg(OH)₂ → MgO + H₂O (on heating)

So all of the magnesium ends up as MgO before the final weighing.
